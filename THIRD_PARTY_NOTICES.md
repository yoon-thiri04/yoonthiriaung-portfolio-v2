# Third-party assets

Technology logos: Devicon v2.17.0, https://github.com/devicons/devicon (MIT). Logos retain their owners' trademarks and identify the named technologies. Downloaded SVGs are served locally in `public/icons/`.

Telegram, Facebook, and Gradio icons: Simple Icons v15.0.0, https://simpleicons.org/ (CC0), served locally.

Fonts: Allura (local script font), Space Grotesk, Manrope, and Noto Sans Myanmar, served through Google Fonts. These use the SIL Open Font License. System-font fallbacks keep content readable if a font cannot load.

The iCAD Sx icon was supplied by Yoon Thiri Aung and is served unchanged in `public/icons/icad-sx.png`. The PyTorch logo is from Devicon.

All portfolio photos, certificates, and application mockups were supplied by Yoon Thiri Aung. The original uploads are preserved without modification. Optimized display derivatives are identified in `asset-inventory.json`.
